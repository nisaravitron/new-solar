Overview

Our Understanding & Goal

Plaza Sky Travel serves customers in Qatar through a bilingual (English & Arabic) website. The next step is turning that website into a steady sales engine through social media and paid ads.

🎯

Primary Goal

Increase enquiries, WhatsApp leads and confirmed bookings (flights, hotel packages, holiday tours, visas, Umrah/Hajj & corporate travel).

🌍

Target Audience

Qatari nationals, expatriate communities (Indian, Pakistani, Filipino, Nepali, Egyptian etc.), families, corporate clients and seasonal travellers.

🗣️

Bilingual Reach

Content, captions and ads in both English and Arabic so that we speak to every customer in their own language.

Strategy

Our Sales-Focused Funnel

Every post and every rupee of ad spend is mapped to a stage of the customer journey, so that activity turns into bookings, not just likes.

1. Awareness

Destination reels, travel tips, festival & season campaigns, Arabic + English creatives to reach new people.

2. Interest

Package carousels, customer stories, "why book with us" videos, visa & travel guides.

3. Enquiry

Lead-generation & Click-to-WhatsApp ads, Google Search ads on high-intent keywords, limited-time offers.

4. Booking & Repeat

Retargeting, follow-up messaging, reviews, referral offers and festive reminders.

Advertising

Ad Channels We Will Run

📘

Meta Ads

Facebook & Instagram campaigns for leads, WhatsApp messages and website traffic, with audience targeting by country, language, interest and travel behaviour.

Google Ads

Search ads on high-intent keywords like "cheap flights Doha", "holiday packages from Qatar", "visa services Qatar", in English and Arabic, plus Display and YouTube.

Roadmap

First 90 Days Plan

 

Week 1 – Audit & Setup

Account access, profile optimisation, brand kit, competitor research, tracking (Pixel / GA4 / Google Ads), content calendar and first-month strategy.

Weeks 2–4 – Launch

Regular posting begins, first ad campaigns go live, A/B testing of creatives, offers and audiences, WhatsApp lead handling process set.

Month 2 – Optimise

Pause low performers, scale winning ads, launch retargeting, add seasonal offers and customer-review content.

Month 3 – Scale

Increase budget on best campaigns, lookalike audiences, referral campaign and next-quarter growth roadmap.
